MusicHub handbook

A conceptual guide to how MusicHub is intended to work for a future user or stakeholder. Each chapter states what is agreed, a conceptual journey, and what is still open.

Chapters

  1. Library and importing
    Where MusicHub keeps music, how music gets in, and who owns the library.
  2. Identification and review
    How MusicHub decides what a file is, and what happens when it is unsure.
  3. Wishlist and acquisition
    How the user says what they want and how MusicHub goes and gets it.
  4. Quality upgrades and versions
    How MusicHub improves audio quality over time without losing what it had.
  5. Scheduler and problem dashboard
    How background work is shown and controlled, and where things that need attention show up.

Cross-cutting principles

  • MusicHub manages its own library and never scans the original NAS collection.
  • The user curates the wishlist; recommendations never authorize acquisition on their own.
  • Identity confidence and audio quality are separate; manual confirmation outranks automation.
  • Nothing is silently deleted or substituted; superseded versions are retained.
  • Everything is configurable without the UI.

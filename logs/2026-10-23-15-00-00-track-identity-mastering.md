# Audible edits define separate catalog tracks

Status: Approved
Date: 2026-10-23

## Decision
A track represents one particular audible audio version. A remaster, remix, alternate mix, live performance, added or changed sound, or other deliberate alteration to the audio content is a distinct catalog track, even when the underlying song/performance is shared. Merely transcoding the same audio to a different format, codec, bitrate or sample rate does not create a new track. Multiple files of the same audio version can reference one track. Different remasters remain separate tracks.

## Context and consequence
This clarifies and supersedes any prior ambiguity that remasters of the same performance may be one track. Audio quality and file encoding remain separate from catalog identity. Album editions must refer to the exact track version present on the selected release; no silent substitution of a remaster or original master. Identity matching should not claim a new version solely due to transcoding artifacts.
